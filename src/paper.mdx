<Section id="takeaway" tone="alt" title="Key Takeaway" center>

<div class="takeaway">

<p>Camera-frame 3D human pose is fundamentally an information decoupling problem: learned RGB priors recover body articulation, while geometric constraints anchor physical scale and sensor placement. By unifying these roles in a single model, <b>MetricPose</b> delivers high metric precision whenever depth or ground cues exist, while retaining full functionality on standard RGB alone.</p>

</div>
